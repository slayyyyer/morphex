Benchmarks

JMH 1.37, two forks, five 500 ms measurements, GC profiler. Inputs are the fixed, versioned benchmark-workload-v1.tsv; they are not sampled at run time. Raw data: results/release-1.2.0/benchmarks/jmh-workload-v1.json and the targeted optimization rerun optimization-unknown-compound.json. Methodology: docs/benchmarks.md.

Measured claim: repeated lookup is 1.36× faster (81.4 vs 110.9 ns) and exact lexical hits are 1.18× faster (100.7 vs 118.5 ns). The optimized unknown and compound paths are 1.49× and 1.13× faster than the release baseline, but remain 7.57× and 4.62× slower than evo2; compacted, uppercase and unsupported paths are also slower (up to 7.9× on uppercase). The optimized rows are a separate targeted rerun. No general speedup is claimed.

The instance is immutable and thread-safe, but concurrency throughput is lower in the same run. Startup was slower than the historical 426 ms median; figures are not mixed across runs.

Reproduce

./mvnw -q -pl inflector-benchmarks -am package
java -jar inflector-benchmarks/target/benchmarks.jar \
  -f 2 -wi 3 -i 5 -w 500ms -r 500ms -prof gc \
  -rf json -rff results/release-1.2.0/benchmarks/jmh-workload-v1.json

The workload covers lexical hits, compacted regular words, unknown inputs, uppercase inference, compounds and unsupported inputs. Historical corpus-only runs are preserved but are not comparable to this fixed-category workload.
